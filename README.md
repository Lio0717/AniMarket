# AniMarket

**A Local Crop Price and Buyer Connector**

## Project Overview

AniMarket is a mobile application concept aligned with **United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 2 (SDG 2): Zero Hunger**. It aims to connect small-scale farmers with potential buyers by providing a platform where farmers can publish crop listings and buyers can browse available agricultural products, review listed prices, and send purchase inquiries.

The application is intended to make local crop information easier to discover and support communication between farmers and buyers.

## Problem Statement

Small-scale farmers may have difficulty reaching potential buyers and sharing updated information about available crops, quantities, and asking prices. At the same time, buyers may find it difficult to discover local suppliers, compare available listings, and contact farmers using scattered sources of information.

AniMarket aims to address these challenges through a centralized mobile application that supports crop discovery, listing management, price information, and initial buyer–farmer communication.

## Target Users

- **Small-Scale Farmers:** Create and manage crop listings, share asking prices and availability information, and review purchase inquiries.
- **Individual and Household Buyers:** Browse local crop listings, review crop details and prices, and contact farmers.
- **Business Buyers:** Discover potential local suppliers, review available crop information, and send inquiries about purchase requirements.

## Core Features

1. User registration and authentication.
2. Role selection for Farmer and Buyer accounts.
3. Farmer crop and harvest listing creation.
4. Crop listing management and editing.
5. Buyer crop browsing and discovery.
6. Crop details, including available price, unit, location, and farmer information.
7. Crop price comparison using clearly identified price sources and units.
8. Purchase inquiries from buyers to farmers.
9. Farmer inquiry review and response, if included in the implemented scope.
10. Profile and account settings.

*Features are proposed and may change as development progresses. Payment, delivery booking, transaction processing, and verified market-price feeds should not be considered available unless they are implemented and tested.*

## Disclaimer

AniMarket is a mobile application project in development. Crop listings, asking prices, availability, market reference prices, inquiries, and other marketplace functions depend on information provided by users and features implemented by the project team.

Farmer-submitted asking prices should not automatically be treated as verified market prices. Any market reference price displayed should identify its source and update date. Proposed features should not be considered fully operational until they have been developed and tested.
