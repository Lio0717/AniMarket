## 2. Team AI Disclosure Audit Log

### Week 1: Ideation, Research, and Project Planning

- *Date & Milestone:* 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 | Week 1 — Problem Framing, Research, User Personas, and Low-Fidelity Wireframes
- *AI Tool & Model:* ChatGPT (GPT-6)
- *Member Responsible:* Gerald Julio A. Digamon & Austine John M. Gonzales

**Prompt Guidance:**

Asked ChatGPT for guidance on organizing the AniMarket mobile application project. Requested support in defining the application's purpose, aligning the project with SDG 2: Zero Hunger, identifying potential farmer and buyer users, planning research and validation, developing preliminary user personas, and organizing the user flow and low-fidelity wireframes.

**AI Output Summary:**

ChatGPT provided suggestions for structuring the project documentation and identifying possible user needs. The guidance focused on small-scale farmers' ability to share crop listings and asking prices, buyers' ability to discover local agricultural products, and the need for clear communication through purchase inquiries.

**Human Refinements & Edits:**

We used the guidance to establish AniMarket as a local crop price and buyer connector application that connects small-scale farmers with potential buyers. We focused on helping farmers publish and manage their crop listings, provide asking prices and availability, and allowing buyers to browse local agricultural products, compare available prices, and send purchase inquiries. We applied relevant suggestions when organizing our Week 1 tasks and planning our initial Figma wireframes based on the project's objectives, target users, and requirements.

**Key Learning Takeaway:**

We learned how to organize the initial stages of a mobile application project, identify a problem related to UN Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger, and determine the needs of small-scale farmers and potential buyers. We also learned how to plan user personas, map user flows, and develop low-fidelity wireframes before proceeding to detailed UI design. Furthermore, we recognized the importance of reviewing and adapting AI-generated guidance to ensure that it aligns with AniMarket's actual objectives, target users, and project requirements.

### Week 2: Component Design System & Clickable Interactive Figma Prototype

- *Date & Milestone:* 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 | Week 2 — Component Design System and Clickable Interactive Figma Prototype
- *AI Tool & Model:* ChatGPT (GPT-6)
- *Member Responsible:* Gerald Julio A. Digamon & Austine John M. Gonzales

**Prompt Guidance:**

Asked ChatGPT for guidance on improving the AniMarket Figma interface, organizing reusable components, applying a consistent dark nature-inspired color palette, arranging frames into logical user journeys, and connecting the buyer and farmer screens in a clickable prototype.

**AI Output Summary:**

ChatGPT suggested a reusable design system and interface components suited to AniMarket, including crop listing cards, price displays, price comparison components, availability badges, inquiry forms, farmer listing forms, navigation components, and feedback messages. It also provided guidance for organizing authentication, buyer, farmer, profile, and logout flows.

**Human Refinements & Edits:**

We improved our AniMarket Figma prototype by maintaining a consistent dark green, nature-inspired design, organizing reusable UI components, and improving navigation across the farmer and buyer interfaces. We also refined the application's key features, including crop listings, price displays, crop details, and purchase inquiries, to provide a more organized and user-friendly experience.

**Key Learning Takeaway:**

Reusable components and consistent styling improve AniMarket's usability by creating a uniform appearance across the application. They also make navigation between the farmer and buyer interfaces easier, provide a more organized user experience, and help maintain consistency throughout the Figma prototype.
