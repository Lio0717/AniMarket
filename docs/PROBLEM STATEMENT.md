# AniMarket: Problem Statement

**Project:** AniMarket — Local Crop Price and Buyer Connector

**UN Sustainable Development Goal:** SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

## 1. Background of the Problem

Agriculture plays an important role in providing food and supporting the livelihoods of communities. Small-scale farmers contribute to local food production, yet they may encounter challenges when promoting their agricultural products, finding potential buyers, and communicating information about their available harvests. Limited access to convenient marketing platforms can make it difficult for farmers to reach a wider group of customers and present their products effectively.

On the other hand, individuals, households, and business operators who want to purchase locally produced crops may experience difficulties finding nearby farmers, identifying available agricultural products, and comparing asking prices. Information about crop availability, product quantities, and prices may be scattered across different communication channels, making the process of finding and contacting potential suppliers less convenient.

These challenges highlight the need for a more accessible and organized way to connect local farmers with potential buyers and make agricultural product information easier to discover.

## 2. Problem Statement

Small-scale farmers need an accessible platform where they can showcase their available agricultural products, provide asking prices, update crop availability, and connect with interested buyers. Similarly, buyers need a convenient way to discover local crops, review product information, compare available asking prices, and communicate directly with farmers.

Without an organized platform connecting these two groups, farmers may have difficulty reaching potential customers, while buyers may spend additional time searching for local agricultural products and gathering information from different sources.

AniMarket aims to address this problem by providing a mobile application that connects farmers and buyers through crop listings, price information, product availability, and purchase inquiries in one platform.

## 3. Target Users

- **Small-Scale Farmers:** Individuals who produce and sell agricultural products and need a convenient way to publish crop listings, display asking prices, manage product availability, and receive inquiries from potential buyers.
- **Buyers:** Individuals, households, and business operators who want to discover locally available agricultural products, review crop information, compare listed prices, and send purchase inquiries to farmers.

## 4. Proposed Solution

AniMarket is a mobile application designed to connect small-scale farmers with potential buyers by providing an organized platform for discovering and presenting local agricultural products. Its proposed features include:

1. **Farmer Crop Listing Management** — Allows farmers to publish, update, and manage information about their available agricultural products.
2. **Crop Information and Details** — Displays relevant product information, including crop names, descriptions, photographs, asking prices, measurement units, and availability.
3. **Browse and Search Crops** — Enables buyers to explore available agricultural products and find crops that match their purchasing needs.
4. **Crop Price Comparison** — Allows buyers to compare available farmer-provided asking prices for similar agricultural products.
5. **Purchase Inquiry** — Provides buyers with a way to express interest in a crop listing and communicate their inquiries to the respective farmer.
6. **Inquiry Management** — Allows farmers to review and respond to inquiries received from interested buyers.

## 5. Project Goal

The goal of AniMarket is to provide a convenient and organized digital platform that connects small-scale farmers with potential buyers, improves access to local agricultural product information, and creates more opportunities for farmers to showcase and promote their available harvests.

## 6. Alignment with UN SDG 2: Zero Hunger

AniMarket supports **UN Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger** by focusing on small-scale food producers and improving access to information about locally available agricultural products. Through its crop listing, price information, and buyer inquiry features, the application aims to help farmers present their products to potential customers and make it easier for buyers to discover local agricultural sources.

The project is particularly aligned with the goal of supporting small-scale food producers by providing a digital platform that may help improve their visibility and access to potential markets. AniMarket aims to complement existing agricultural selling practices rather than replace traditional markets, established trading systems, or government agricultural services.

## 7. Scope and Limitations

AniMarket focuses on connecting small-scale farmers and buyers through crop listings, product information, farmer-provided asking prices, availability updates, price comparison, and purchase inquiries. The application is intended to make local agricultural product information more accessible and organize communication between its two main user groups.

The accuracy of crop listings, asking prices, and availability information depends on the information provided and updated by farmers. Price comparisons are based on available listings and should not automatically be considered official or verified market prices. The availability of agricultural products may change depending on harvest conditions and farmer updates.

The application serves as a platform for product discovery and purchase inquiries. It does not guarantee the completion of transactions, product quality, or the accuracy of all submitted information. Payment processing, delivery arrangements, and logistics services are not included unless separately implemented as part of the system.

## 8. Expected Impact

AniMarket aims to improve the accessibility of local agricultural product information and provide small-scale farmers with another opportunity to showcase their available harvests. By bringing crop listings, asking prices, availability information, and purchase inquiries together in one platform, the application hopes to make it easier for buyers to discover local agricultural products and connect with potential suppliers.

The project also aims to encourage the use of digital technology in local agricultural marketing, strengthen communication between farmers and buyers, and support opportunities for small-scale food producers. Through these efforts, AniMarket hopes to contribute to more accessible local agricultural markets and the broader objectives of SDG 2: Zero Hunger.

## 9. Conclusion

AniMarket is designed to address the challenges of connecting small-scale farmers with potential buyers by providing an accessible platform for presenting, discovering, and inquiring about local agricultural products. Through its proposed features, the application aims to make crop information easier to access, improve the visibility of farmers' available harvests, and provide buyers with a more convenient way to find local agricultural products.

Aligned with UN SDG 2: Zero Hunger, the project emphasizes the importance of supporting small-scale food producers and improving their opportunities to reach potential markets. By using a mobile platform to connect farmers and buyers, AniMarket aims to contribute to a more organized and accessible local agricultural marketplace.
